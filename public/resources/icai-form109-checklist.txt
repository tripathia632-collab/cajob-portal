--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
OVERVIEW OF TRANSFER PERMISSIBILITY UNDER ICAI NORMS:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
1. FIRST YEAR OF ARTICLESHIP:
   • Unconditional transfer is permitted during the 1st year of articleship training.
   • Neither the consent of the Principal nor ICAI prior approval is required.
   • Form 109 must be executed and submitted via the Self Service Portal (SSP).

2. SECOND YEAR OF ARTICLESHIP:
   • General transfer is restricted unless falling under permitted grounds:
     a) Medical grounds of the trainee or immediate family.
     b) Relocation/shifting of family residence to another city (more than 50 km).
     c) Death or permanent disability of the Principal / closure of firm.
     d) Moving to Industrial Training in an approved financial corporate.
     e) Mutual agreement with unconditional NOC from existing Principal.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP-BY-STEP PROCEDURE ON ICAI SSP PORTAL:
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
[ ] STEP 1: Professional Communication with Principal
    • Discuss transfer plans professionally and transparently with your Principal.
    • Complete all pending audit workpapers, vouching files, and tax return assignments.
    • Return all firm laptops, access cards, client files, and tokens.

[ ] STEP 2: Calculation of Stipend & Attendance
    • Verify that stipend is cleared up to the last working day under ICAI Regulation 48.
    • Calculate total leaves taken vs. maximum leave entitlement (1/6th of total training).
    • Confirm that excess leaves are documented or adjusted properly.

[ ] STEP 3: Initiation on ICAI Self Service Portal (SSP)
    • Log into eservices.icai.org using your Student credentials.
    • Navigate to "Article Functions" -> "Articleship Termination Form 109".
    • Enter the exact date of termination.
    • Input work experience details across Audit, Taxation, and Accounting.

[ ] STEP 4: Principal Approval & Digital Signature
    • The form moves to the Principal's SSP dashboard.
    • Principal reviews work done, leaves taken, and approves Form 109.

[ ] STEP 5: Registration with New Firm (Form 102 & 103)
    • Form 102 (Deed of Articles) executed on non-judicial stamp paper with new Principal.
    • Form 103 submitted on SSP within 30 days of joining the new firm.
